Channel: Cold Case Files - English-language true crime documentary channel for a US audience.
Rules for this script:
- Stick to documented facts (court records, official investigations, reputable reporting). Clearly mark theories as theories.
- For living people, only state what courts or official records established. No rumors.
- No graphic gore: describe violence briefly and with restraint (advertiser-friendly).
- Drug cases: focus on the investigation, the people, the consequences and the victims. Never explain how to produce, process, hide or smuggle drugs, and never glamorize the trade.
- Respect victims: name them, give them a life before the crime.
- Open with the most gripping unanswered question, not with dates.
